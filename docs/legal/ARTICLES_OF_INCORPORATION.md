### TENTH:
Dissolution and Liquidation:

1. **Event of Dissolution**: The LLC shall be dissolved upon occurrence of any of the following:
   - Written agreement of all Members to dissolve
   - Death or incapacity of the sole Manager (without successor designated)
   - Breach of Delaware LLC statute or failure to maintain registered agent

2. **Liquidation Process**:
   - Appoint liquidator (by unanimous Member agreement)
   - Settle creditor claims and obligations
   - Distribute remaining assets per Members' ownership percentages
   - File Certificate of Cancellation with Delaware Secretary of State

3. **Blockchain Continuity**: In the event of LLC dissolution, the blockchain network infrastructure may be transferred to a successor entity or decentralized governance structure by Manager discretion.

## NOTES FOR LEGAL TEAM:

1. **Registered Agent**: File with a Delaware registered agent service (costs ~$100–$300/year). Examples: CT Corporation System, Legalzoom, Registered Agent LLC.

2. **EIN**: After formation, obtain Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) from IRS.

3. **Operating Agreement**: A detailed Operating Agreement (separate document, not the Certificate) should cover:
   - Profit/loss sharing
   - Voting procedures
   - Capital calls
   - Transfer restrictions
   - Buyout provisions

4. **Multi-State Compliance**: If operating outside Delaware, may need to register as a foreign LLC in each state where business is conducted.

5. **Regulatory Consultation**: Consult with tax attorney and CPA regarding:
   - Pass-through tax treatment (LLC typically taxed as disregarded entity or partnership)
   - State sales tax obligations (if selling goods/services)
   - Commodity regulations (if SPRC token issuance triggers CFTC authority)
   - Securities regulations (if offering equity or revenue-sharing to Members)

6. **Blockchain-Specific Issues**:
   - Clarify whether SPRC token constitutes a "security" under SEC Rule 10b-5
   - Document technical controls for private key management
   - Establish contingency plan for protocol upgrades and hard forks
   - Define liability cap for blockchain service failures

# Brand Guide

## Logos

Provide square and horizontal logos in SVG and PNG formats.

- Primary: `assets/logo-primary.svg`, `assets/logo-primary.png`
- Monochrome: `assets/logo-mono.svg`, `assets/logo-mono.png`

## Usage

- Maintain clear space around the logo.
- Do not alter colors or aspect ratio.

## Colors

- Primary: #2C3E50
- Accent: #3498DB
